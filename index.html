I am an Economics graduate with professional experience in treasury and financial markets. At COFIDE, I participate in the analysis of short-term money-market operations and the monitoring of sovereign and global fixed-income portfolios.

I am particularly interested in sovereign fixed income. I enjoy following rates, central banks, inflation expectations, fiscal developments and institutional flows, and trying to understand how all of them become actual trades and changes in bond prices.

A significant part of my learning has also come from conversations with professionals working directly in financial markets. I value these relationships because they allow me to contrast academic frameworks with how investment decisions are actually made.

I am also exploring how artificial intelligence and software can be used to build financial research tools, trading simulations and independent products. My objective is to keep developing projects that are useful, technically interesting and potentially scalable.

Peruvian Sovereign Yield Curve in 3D

Interactive reconstruction of the Peruvian sovereign yield curve across time using residual maturities, PCHIP interpolation and Nelson–Siegel parametrization.

The visualization makes it possible to examine changes in curve level, slope and curvature and to identify periods of unusual dislocation in the long end.

Python / Plotly / SciPy / Nelson–Siegel

Peruvian Sovereign Bond Issuance

Historical database and interactive analysis of domestic sovereign bond issuance by Peru's Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Sovereign Bond Trading Desk Simulator

An experimental fixed-income trading simulator built around a dynamic sovereign yield curve, institutional order flow, macroeconomic events and market microstructure.

The project explores how AFPs, insurers, banks and offshore investors may interact under different market regimes and how those flows translate into duration, DV01 and P&L.
