My main professional interest is fixed income and sovereign bond markets. Through my work I have gained exposure to the trading and analysis of Peruvian sovereign securities, global bonds, FX markets and institutional fixed-income transactions.

What attracts me most to markets is the combination of macroeconomics, quantitative analysis and human behavior. I enjoy studying how inflation expectations, monetary policy, fiscal developments, positioning and market liquidity translate into movements across the yield curve.

I have also been fortunate to build relationships with professionals from whom I continuously learn about trading, portfolio management and financial markets. I place a high value on learning from people with direct market experience and on continuously improving my own analytical framework.

Fixed income is more than a professional area of interest for me. I genuinely enjoy studying bond markets, following rates, understanding market narratives and developing a deeper intuition for how institutional investors make decisions.

Alongside markets, I am increasingly interested in the use of artificial intelligence and software development to build independent financial tools and products. My objective is to combine financial market knowledge, quantitative methods and AI to create useful projects that can eventually become scalable independent ventures.

Selected projects

Fixed Income
Yield Curve

Peruvian sovereign yield curve in 3D

Interactive visualization of the Peruvian sovereign yield curve between 2018 and 2026, constructed across residual maturities using PCHIP interpolation and Nelson–Siegel parametrization. The project allows the evolution of curve level, slope and curvature to be examined through time.

Peruvian sovereign bond issuance · 2001–2026

Data pipeline and historical analysis of domestic sovereign bond issuance by Peru's Ministry of Economy and Finance. The project includes extraction from public sources, validation against official figures and interactive analysis of issuance by maturity segment.

Sovereign Bond Trading Desk Simulator

Institutional fixed-income market simulator combining a dynamic sovereign yield curve, macroeconomic events, institutional agents, order flow and market microstructure. The project explores duration, DV01, liquidity, positioning and P&L across changing market regimes.
